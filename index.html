What is the AI Navigator?

Finding the right AI tool should start with your work, not with a list of products. Explore realistic tasks, see a recommended starting point, and follow practical steps. These mappings are proposed guidance, not an automatic access or data-use authorization.

General rule: Start with institutional Copilot for most everyday AI tasks. Use a specialized tool—or Claude Enterprise—when a particular capability makes it better suited to the job.

How to use it

  1. Choose a work category and the task closest to yours.
  2. Review the starting tool and its Get started instructions.
  3. Use the optional data-classification examples when unsure what information is involved.
  4. Explore Tools for more detail or Glossary for terminology.

The Details/Tools/Glossary panel can be resized or hidden.
