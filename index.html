What is the AI Navigator?

As institutionally available AI tools expand, it can be hard to know what fits your work. Explore common tasks and situations, compare suggested tools, and find the guidance needed to use AI responsibly. This is a navigation aid—not a substitute for current institutional approvals.

How to use it

  1. Optionally select your role to put relevant categories first.
  2. Choose a type of work, then a situation.
  3. Compare tools in Details. Open the Glossary for unfamiliar terms.
  4. Resize the sidebar by dragging its edge, or hide and reopen it.
