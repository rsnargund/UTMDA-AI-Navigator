What is the AI Navigator?

As the number of available AI tools grows, finding the right one for your work can be difficult. This guide organizes common situations, explains which tools may fit, and shows how to get started. The categories and tool matches are proposed refinements of the supplied decision guide, not an institutional approval list. Confirm current permissions and approved uses.

How to use it

  1. Optionally choose your role to prioritize relevant tasks.
  2. Select a type of work and the situation that fits.
  3. Most tasks give you a starting tool immediately. For some writing and coding tasks, choose whether your materials are public or institutional.
  4. Follow the Get started steps; browse Tools or Glossary for more detail.
